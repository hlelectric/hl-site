Electrical work across Bossier City

H&L Electrical Services is a family-owned electrical shop based in Ringgold, and Bossier City is a regular part of our service area. We handle the everyday repairs that keep a house running, plus the upgrades people put off until something goes wrong.

Bossier has a mix of established neighborhoods and newer subdivisions, and each comes with its own electrical needs. Older homes often need panel work, extra circuits or a fix for wiring that’s been added onto over the years. Newer homes tend to call us for ceiling fans, outdoor outlets, lighting changes and EV chargers in the garage.

Small jobs welcome

Have one outlet that quit working, or a breaker that trips every time the microwave and toaster run together? That’s exactly the kind of call we take. We’ll track down the reason instead of just resetting things, explain what we found, and fix it.

What we do around the house

  • Troubleshooting: dead outlets, tripping breakers, flickering lights, a room with no power
  • Ceiling fans, light fixtures and dimmers, indoors and on porches and patios
  • Panel upgrades and Federal Pacific or fuse panel replacement
  • EV charger installs for Tesla and all major Level 2 brands
  • Outlets and switches, LED lighting (including LED flat panels for offices and shops), underground wiring and remodel electrical (see all our services)

Homes and businesses

We do residential and commercial work, including swapping old fluorescent lay-in fixtures for LED flat panels in offices and shops.
